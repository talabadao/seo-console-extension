# SEO Console — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-16_

SEO Console is a browser extension that analyzes the SEO of the web page you are
viewing and shows the results in a side panel.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** SEO Console does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any
personal information or browsing data. We have no servers and no analytics.

## How your data is used

All analysis happens locally, inside your browser:

- The extension reads the content and response details of the page in your active
  tab (HTML, meta tags, headings, images, links, HTTP status and headers, Core Web
  Vitals) only to display SEO diagnostics to you.
- The broken-link checker and the optional site crawl request URLs from the sites
  you are inspecting, directly from your browser, to check whether they respond.
  These requests are not routed through us and are not logged.
- Your preferences (theme and rule thresholds) are saved using the browser's local
  `storage` and never leave your device.

None of this data is sent to the developer or to any third party. It is not sold,
and it is not used for advertising, creditworthiness, or any purpose unrelated to
providing the SEO analysis you requested.

## Permissions

The extension requests broad host access and `webRequest` so it can read the
status code, redirect chain and response headers of the page you analyze and check
your links. This access is used solely for on-page SEO analysis within your
browser.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on the project's repository.
